---

## Scope

This reason code applies when the same transaction was processed more than once,
or when the cardholder paid for the same goods or services by another method and
was charged again on the card. It is a processing error dispute, not a fraud or
delivery dispute: the cardholder recognises the merchant and does not contest the
underlying purchase.

## Duplicate identification

A duplicate is established by two transactions from the same merchant for the
same amount, on the same or adjacent dates, for the same goods or services. Two
transactions of equal value on the same day are not automatically duplicates —
a cardholder may legitimately buy the same item twice, and the merchant may
represent by showing two distinct orders.

## Paid by other means

Where the cardholder claims payment by another method, the issuer must supply
evidence of that other payment: a cash receipt, a bank transfer reference or a
second card transaction. A bare assertion is not sufficient because the issuer
alone cannot establish that the two payments covered the same obligation.

## Merchant representment evidence

The merchant represents by showing that the two transactions were separate,
that one was already refunded, or that the alternative payment cited by the
cardholder relates to a different obligation. A refund already issued for one of
the two transactions resolves the dispute.

## Time limits

The issuer must raise the dispute within 120 calendar days of the later of the
two transaction processing dates. The merchant has 30 calendar days to
represent.
