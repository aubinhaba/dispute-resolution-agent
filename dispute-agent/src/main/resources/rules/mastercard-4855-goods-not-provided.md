title: Goods or Services Not Provided

## Scope

This message reason code applies when the cardholder engaged in the transaction
but the merchant did not provide the goods or services. It covers non-delivery
and merchant failure to perform, including the case where a merchant has ceased
trading before fulfilling an order. Identity is not disputed.

## Waiting period

The issuer may not raise this dispute before the agreed delivery or performance
date has passed. Where no date was agreed, the issuer must wait 30 calendar days
from the transaction date. A dispute raised inside the waiting period is invalid
on procedure and the acquirer may represent on that basis alone, without
addressing the substance.

## Cardholder statement

The issuer must hold a statement identifying what was purchased, the date
delivery or performance was expected, and confirmation that it did not occur.
Where the merchant has ceased trading, the issuer may substitute evidence of the
cessation for the cardholder's contact attempt.

## Acquirer representment evidence

The acquirer represents with proof that the goods or services were provided.
For physical goods this means a carrier record of delivery. For services it
means a record showing the service was rendered or made available. For travel
and events it means proof that the booking was honoured or that the cardholder
was offered a credit consistent with the terms accepted at purchase.

## Credits and vouchers

Where the merchant issued a credit, a voucher or a rebooking that the cardholder
accepted, the acquirer may represent with that evidence. A voucher the cardholder
did not accept does not discharge the obligation, and the dispute stands.

## Time limits

The issuer must submit the dispute within 120 calendar days of the latest of the
transaction date, the expected delivery date, or the date the cardholder learned
that delivery would not occur, up to a maximum of 540 days. The acquirer has 45
calendar days to submit a second presentment.

## Common outcomes

Second presentment succeeds with an independent carrier delivery record or a
documented accepted credit. It fails when the acquirer offers only merchant-side
fulfilment status, or when the dispute was raised after a valid delivery that the
merchant simply failed to evidence in time.
